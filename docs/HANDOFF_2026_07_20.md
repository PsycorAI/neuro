## Recent findings & pointers

- **Spike control works at λ=1.0:** plateaued 0.32–0.34 after step 46k restart; val_ppl trade-off
  (~+5 ppl) acceptable vs. unbound creep. See: [[neuro_500m_v3_sparsity_tuning]]
- **Batch=14 is the VRAM sweet-spot on this box:** batch=16 overflows into WDDM shared memory,
  halves throughput (10.9k→6.9k) despite 95% GPU util (stalled SMs). See: [[neuro_500m_v3_vram_sweetspot]]
- **MQAR N pinning to 16** killed the 29-shape recompile fragmentation (the root cause of earlier
  creep). See: [[reference_torch_compile_delta]], journal §45
- **Expandable_segments removed:** crashed with `CUDA driver error: device not ready` on WSL2/WDDM.
  Low-level CUDA VM APIs unstable there. See: [[neuro_500m_v3_vram_sweetspot]]

## Gotchas

- **eval defragments the allocator:** every eval's smaller batch (~200 small forwards + empty_cache)
  cleans up VRAM fragmentation, temporarily enabling higher batch (e.g., batch=16 briefly post-eval).
  This is benign if unspectacular; don't design around it.
- **Mid-run hyperparameter tweaks are slow to read:** λ=0.7→1.0 took 42 evals (~39k steps, ~7 hours)
  to confirm effect. Avoid unless critical. Post-pretrain refinement is cheaper.

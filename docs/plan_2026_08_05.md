# Post-fix plan — 2026-08-05

Supersedes the "Next steps (in order)" list in `to-do.html`'s Immediate Plan
and the Step 3 seed-variance framing in `step3_midscale_design.md` §Phase 2.
Companion to `HANDOFF_2026_08_05.md`.

## Context that reshaped the plan

Step 3's diagnosed failure was **stability, not seed variance**. Fixes are in:
Muon decoupled weight decay (root cause) + `gdn2_gate_norm` RMSNorm (symptom
cover). Arm B (gn+wd, hot lr, batch 16/ga2) is live at step 6000 — val_ppl
60.65 monotonically descending, spike 0.149, dstd 0.8–1.3, at_floor 0% on
every block through the historical divergence zone.

Three verified facts changed the plan:

1. **Gate-norm costs 38% throughput.** Same shape, same batch 28, same
   compile: `step3diag_lam1` 13,187 tok/s (gn off) vs `step3diag_gatenorm`
   8,011 tok/s (gn on). Arm B's batch 16/ga2 recovers to 8,433 tok/s — so
   the batch change is essentially free; **gate-norm is the entire tax**.
2. **The wd-only arm was never run.** Arms were gn+wd, gn+wd-hot, gn-only.
   The valuable diagnostic — does wd alone hold? — is missing. Root-cause fix
   costs nothing; symptom fix costs 38%. Answering this is worth 4 hours.
3. **Coherence / generative quality / empirical energy / plasticity have
   broken instruments.** `lm_eval.py`, `generate.py`, `empirical_energy.py`,
   `persistent_brain_demo.py`, `brain_domain_transfer.py`,
   `stateful_eval.py`, `spec_decode.py` all hand-roll `SpikingHebbianLM(...)`
   and are missing every post-July flag (gdn2, attention, qk_norm,
   gate_norm). This is how 500M v3 reached demo day before anyone
   discovered it couldn't chat.

Two arithmetic facts also apply:

- Corpus is 14.5B tokens (`/home/glenn/projects/bdh/data/tokenized/train.bin`,
  58.1 GB uint32). We routinely use 1.1B — 7.6% of the data. Data is not the
  constraint; compute-per-token is.
- No architecture in the promoted-idea backlog survived triage (SDM,
  Fracconn, uniform-MoR, MoE all closed-negative; per-token MoR needs unbuilt
  kernel work). The remaining quality is in tokens, context length, and
  measurement — not new blocks.

## What this plan is *not*

- Not a 3-seed × 1.1B-token variance study. Original Step 3 was scoped
  before the stability failure; that failure is now the more informative
  signal, and 30M+90M both cleared 3-seed post-QK-norm. 2-seed at 400M is
  sufficient at 276M for a decision.
- Not a new architecture. Additive architectural changes are deferred
  until the flagship 363M ships or a specific measurable defect points at
  one.
- Not a scaling commitment to 1B yet. The flagship 363M's job is to be
  the first properly-trained (~Chinchilla-ratio) model in the project *and*
  the last de-risking step before a 1B commit.

**Decision gate:**
- Recall N=4 ≥ ~60%, monotonic decay through N=64, memory-Δ >0. Pass →

  audit before Phase 1 spends any GPU time.

## Phase 1 — settle the recipe (5090, ~17h, one overnight)

All at 276M shape, schedule-faithful (`max_tokens: 1.1B`, wall-clock
truncated), **200M tokens each** (well past the historical step-7000
divergence). One variable per arm; arm B is the control.

| arm | change from arm B | question answered | if it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| **W1** | `gdn2_gate_norm: false`, batch 28 | Is gate-norm needed given wd? | +1.6× throughput on every future run |
| **W2** | W1 + `sparsity_lambda: 0.5` | λ was raised to 1.0 during the divergence panic; wd was the cause, not λ | recovers ~5 ppl per 500M v3 precedent |
| **W3** | winner + `lr: 7e-4` | Step Law wants ~1.0e-3 at 1.1B tokens; we're at 0.5× | closes the undertraining gap for the flagship |
| **W4** | winner + `grad_accum: 2` at effective batch 28×2=56 | Step Law wants ~84k tok/step; batch 28 is 6× under. Larger batch is also stabilizing | ppl + stability margin at scale |

Each arm gets one 200M-token run at 7:1 attention. Chain them with `;` (not
`&&`) so timeout exits don't kill the chain. Configs
`configs/w{1,2,3,4}_recipe.yaml` copied from `step3fix_gnwd_hot_b16.yaml`
with the single field changed.

**Watchdogs (auto-abort):**
- `dstd > 3.0` on any block, two consecutive evals
- any block `at_floor > 20%`
- spike rate rising for two consecutive evals past 0.20

**Decision rules:**
- **W1 pass** (val_ppl descending + at_floor <10% at step 14k + recall
  matches arm B's) → drop gate-norm from the recipe and win the throughput.
- **W1 fail** (divergence pattern returns) → keep gate-norm; the 38% is
  paid. Skip to W2 with gate-norm on.
- **W2 pass** → adopt λ=0.5. **Not** applying the quadratic penalty
  (gotcha #4); W2 tests whether the linear penalty transfers before we
  build machinery to make it transfer.
- **W3 pass** → adopt lr=7e-4 for the flagship. Fail → keep 5e-4; the
  quality upside is smaller than the divergence risk.
- **W4 pass** → adopt larger effective batch. This is the most
  scale-relevant of the four — batch size interacts with everything
  downstream.

The winner recipe = arm B + whichever of W1-W4 pass, composed. Non-composing
combinations (W1 fails so gate-norm stays on) fall back cleanly.

## Phase 3 — surgical hygiene (~2h, same overnight window)

1. **Spike-rate dilution** — `src/model.py:1312-1314`. Attention layers
   push zeros into `spike_accum`; the flat mean under-reports true spiking
   activity by ~14% today, ~28% at 2 attention layers. Fix: average over
   spiking-block spikes only.
   - Restates the sparsity target correctly (0.151 reported ≈ 0.173 true)
   - Un-dilutes the sparsity penalty
   - Prevents future attention-ratio comparisons from being spuriously
     "sparser" per gotcha #5 and `step3_midscale_design.md` §3
2. **Decay-logit std watchdog** — `train_gpu.evaluate()`: warn at dstd >
   2.5, abort at dstd > 3.0 for two consecutive evals. Per gotcha #6, dstd
   is the cleanest early tripwire we have.
3. **Skip `W_beta`/`W_alpha` construction under gdn2** — `src/model.py`
   block init: guard with `if not gdn2_enabled`. Zero training effect
   (dead params today), but eliminates the diagnostic trap that cost 30
   min this session per `neuro_step3_divergence_2026_08_04.md`.

**Not doing:**
- Quadratic sparsity penalty (gotcha #4) — W2 tests linear-λ transfer
  first; simplicity-first.
- Batch-size Step-Law screen as its own arm — folded into W4.
- Code-hygiene P6 (strip dead SDM/OSDN/Titans/Markov/MoR/confidence/
  Engram-v1 branches) — deferred to Phase 4's long-wait window, not
  the overnight.

## Phase 5 — 363M flagship (~8.5 days on 5090)

**Shape:** `d=1280`, `n_neurons=3072`, `d_mem=1280`, `L=9`, head_dim 128,
attention at 7:1 (position `[4]` in a 9-layer stack). 363M params.
Rationale: cleanest ~350M at n≈2.4d, head_dim 128 is the modern
convention, 8-9 layers is the same family the 1B design uses, so this
directly de-risks the 1B scale-up.

**Budget:** **~7B tokens (~19 tok/param)** — the first Chinchilla-ratio
run in this project. 50% of the corpus, no repetition. Doubles as a
serious quality artifact, not just a screen.

**Recipe:** winner from Phase 1 + Phase 3 fixes. Pre-launch mandatory:

1. **~200M-token shakedown** at the new shape (~5h) — new-scale
   breakage is this project's own history (500M v3 spike creep, 276M
   divergence, MoR 200M throughput cliff). Runs the exact recipe with
   watchdogs; must clear the same at_floor / dstd bars as arm B.
2. **VRAM/throughput probe** with all Phase-1 winners composed
   (`temp/probe_363m.py`). If `block_size` 768 or 1024 fits with
   reasonable batch, adopt it — longer context materially strengthens
   BOTH differentiators (energy 1.99×→2.74× per matched-depth recompute;
   persistent-memory recall).
3. **Sparsity-λ ratio audit** — per
   `neuro_sparsity_refine_ratio_gotcha`, match λ as a RATIO to the base's
   own pretrain λ. Do NOT inherit W2's absolute λ if the winning λ was
   tuned at 276M.
4. **`keep_last: 6`** for seed 0 forensics; drop to 1 for any future
   runs after this one lands.

**Success:** measured across all seven quality axes now that instruments
exist. Baseline = Phase 2's 276M numbers.

- **Memory & recall** — recall_eval N=4..64 --replay split; must
  monotonically dominate the 276M baseline
- **Coherence & generative quality** — chat.py prompt suite +
  `persistent_brain_demo.py` (needs SFT+DPO on top before final judgment)
- **Stability** — no watchdog trip through 7B tokens
- **Plasticity** — `brain_domain_transfer.py` cross-domain lift
- **Efficiency/sparsity** — `empirical_energy.py` matched-depth
  (expect 2.7-2.9× per `pre_350m_gotchas.md` §3b extrapolation)
- **Robustness** — `lm_eval.py` HellaSwag/ARC/MMLU (undertrained by
  Chinchilla standards even at 19 tok/param, but comparable across
  checkpoints)
- **Downstream ppl** — `stateful_eval.py`

## After the flagship — 1B decision gate

If the 363M flagship clears all seven axes and Phase 2's baseline shows
clean monotonic scaling from 276M → 363M on recall, memory-Δ, and
downstream ppl, the 1B design (`docs/1b_v1_design.md`) is the next commit.
If any axis fails or scales unfavorably, that failure defines the next
architectural work item — not a hypothetical from the current backlog.

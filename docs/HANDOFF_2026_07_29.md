## Gotchas

- **DO NOT use Pass 2 checkpoint** — recall crushed 40-50% relative for near-zero ppl gain. Point demos + downstream evals at Pass 1 endpoint (`sparsity_cleanup_500m_v3/step_0016388.pt`).
- **Chat quality depends on prompt form** — incomplete-sentence prompts (`"The capital of France is"`) land off-distribution for the SFT stage and produce short truncated wrong answers. Well-formed questions/imperatives (`"What is the capital of France?"`) work. See journey §47 chat-quality diagnostic.
- **Chat default temperature 0.8 is correct** — lower temperatures make the greedy path find shorter/wronger answers, counterintuitively.

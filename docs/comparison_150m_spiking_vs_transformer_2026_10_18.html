Honest caveats

  • The seed-0 first-chunk gap (5.7%) is bigger than seed 1 (2.7%): the fair claim is "about 3–6% lower perplexity inside the window, about 10% lower across windows".
  • Both models use chunked attention (window 1024). A full-attention transformer would close part of the cross-chunk gap; not tested yet (to-do, parked).
  • Energy numbers are a model of the hardware (energy-v2), not chip measurements; the Lava simulator test is the next step.
  • On GPUs the transformer trains 1.55x faster; the spiking advantages are quality, memory, robustness and estimated energy, not GPU speed.
  • Benchmarks (HellaSwag / ARC) are near chance at 150M and do not separate the models.

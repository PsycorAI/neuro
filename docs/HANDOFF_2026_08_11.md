## Gotchas

- **Post-resume VRAM fragmentation:** auto-resume from a flagship checkpoint jumps reserved 26.5→29.2 GB → 0.1 GB WDDM spill → ~20% throughput hit. Sometimes self-recovers after ~10k more steps; sometimes needs pkill + relaunch (~15 min compile) to fully clear. Watch tok/s; if it stays below 7,500 for several evals, restart.
- **Monitor tool's shell env:** `kill -0 <PID>` returns wrong answers (misfired twice as "PROCESS DEAD" while process was clearly alive). Use pure log-based liveness monitors (grep step count over time) — the crash-signature monitor is unaffected and remains reliable.
- **`launch >> log`** not `> log` after the first launch — auto-resume runs preserve the telemetry history across restarts.
- **B=20 spills reliably** — probe hit 27.7 GB reserved with grad_accum=1/no-MQAR; real training pushed dedicated to 30.6 GB and shared to 8.5 GB immediately. B=18 is the ceiling on the 5090 for this shape.

## Gotchas

- **WSD is unsafe with this recipe.** Any WSD reroll needs a lower peak LR
  screened at scale first, or a wnorm growth-rate tripwire — the val_ppl
  tripwire fires late (only after damage lands val_ppl-visible).
- **max-autotune-no-cudagraphs compile is ~30–45 min** and produces zero step
  output until the first `log_every` (1000) hits. A cold-start-to-step-1000
  budget of <60 min will look "stuck" and cause false-alarm watcher timeouts.
- **`log_every=1000` means regex `step 100 \|` will NEVER match** the first
  log line (it prints `step 1000 |`). Any auto-watcher regex needs `step
  1000` or `step (1000|[2-9][0-9]{3,}) \|`.
- **Windows-side Write/Edit onto `\\wsl.localhost\...` strips +x** on scripts
  and can re-introduce CRLF; invoke shell scripts as `bash /path/script.sh`
  and `sed -i 's/\r$//' /path/script.sh` after any Windows-side edit.
- The WSD attempt's checkpoints live at `models/checkpoints/flagship_v2_363m/`
  (do NOT reuse that `run_name` for a new run — it would auto-resume from
  the diverged `step_0018000.pt`).

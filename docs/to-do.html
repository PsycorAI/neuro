  1. GDN-2 — erase/write decoupling in the delta rule (BUILT 2026-07-11, UNBLOCKED 2026-07-12 via grad-ckpt, queued on 5090 follow-on chain) — arXiv:2605.22791, NVLabs. The same paper whose β-collapse diagnosis gave us beta_floor; the floor is a patch, this is the actual fix. Standard gated delta uses a single scalar to control two different things — how much old content to erase (key side) and how much new to commit (value side). GDN-2 splits them into a channel-wise erase gate b_t = σ(W_b x) and channel-wise write gate w_t = σ(W_w x), with channel-wise decay D_t = diag(α_t). Why it fits: targets exactly the multi-key retrieval our MQAR refines couldn't move, and the write gate w_t carries the beta_floor stability lesson forward as a per-channel floor.

Reported +32% in-context recall at 340M (near our scale). Why it fits: pure recall lever, orthogonal to gating (composes with our existing decay_gate + beta_floor), very small code change.

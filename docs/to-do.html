Research-process idea (added 2026-09-25): Dream-RSI (Google, arXiv 2609.14858) trains NO weights. It improves an agent's exploration policy by replaying past discovery trees as free simulators. Our analogue: treat the journey/screen history (screen → outcome) as a replay tree to tune our screening policy (which levers to screen, when to stop a screen early). Our tree is small (~100 nodes), so value is low-to-moderate. PARKED.

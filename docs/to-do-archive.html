Evaluated & rejected — enhancement candidates (as of 2026-07-11)

Recall-lever research verdicts (2026-07-11), items NOT pursued: Erase-then-Delta (arXiv:2606.26560) duplicates GDN-2 — GDN-2 is better validated, pick it. TTT/TNT test-time-training variants overlap what the delta rule already is. Flow-matching, multi-head attention, and full hyper-connections remain rejected (see the line below).

Evaluated & rejected (pre-2026-07-11): Multi-Head Attention (Neuro is deliberately non-attention; delta_rule already fills the role) · Flow-Matching (image/continuous ODE, no fit for AR discrete-token LM) · BLT byte-latent (ground-up tokenizer replacement, incompatible with Phi-3.5+KD pipeline) · DeepSeek V4 Sparse Attention / CSA / HCA / MLA (all attention + KV-cache optimizations — we have neither) · Qwen3-Next hybrid full-attention layers (violates the non-attention thesis; only pays off at very long context). Already done: PMA (keep_last:8 + pma_average.py); Gated DeltaNet (our delta_rule, now also Qwen3-Next's backbone). Already tried & closed negative: Engram v1 (though DeepSeek V4 now runs it at scale — Engram v2 is our fix). Incremental / not worth a dedicated item: 2026 KD refinements (layer-wise / task-aware distillation) — our Phi-3.5 KD already works.
